# Scaling Strategy

Many organizations confuse implementing a tool with executing a strategy. Dashboards are an excellent way to monitor metrics, but the moment the metric becomes the goal, the strategy fails.

> "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure." — Goodhart's Law

We must focus on global maxima, not just localized operational peaks.
