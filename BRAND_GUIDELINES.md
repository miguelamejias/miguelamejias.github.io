# Miguel Ángel Mejía — Brand System & Website Guidelines

---

# 1. Brand Core

## Positioning
Miguel Ángel Mejía is a strategic translator between business and technology, focused on aligning decisions, reducing costly errors, and bringing clarity to complex technological environments.

## Core Statement
Most companies don’t have a technology problem.

They have a decision-making and communication problem.

---

# 2. Value Proposition

Miguel translates technical complexity into clear business decisions, helping organizations avoid costly mistakes and align technology with real outcomes.

---

# 3. Audience

- CTOs
- Tech Leads
- Startup founders
- Traditional companies in digital transformation
- Decision-makers in technology-driven environments

---

# 4. Brand Voice

## Personality Traits

### Strategic
Focuses on decisions, consequences, and impact.
Example:
"If business and technology are not aligned, you are losing money."

### Clear
Simplifies complex ideas into understandable insights.
Example:
"The problem is not technology. It’s misalignment."

### Direct
Communicates without unnecessary complexity.
Example:
"You don’t need more tech. You need better decisions."

### Analytical
Explains root causes and patterns.
Example:
"Projects don’t fail in execution. They fail in definition."

### Authoritative
Speaks from experience, not theory.
Example:
"I’ve seen this pattern repeat across multiple projects."

---

# 5. Tone Variations

## LinkedIn
Tone: Strategic, reflective, direct  
Example: "Most companies think they have a tech problem. They don’t."

## Website
Tone: Structured, authoritative  
Example: "This is not a technical issue. It’s a decision problem."

## Social Media
Tone: Direct, simple, impactful  
Example: "Not tech. Bad decisions."

## Video
Tone: Conversational, firm  
Example: "Let me be clear: this is costing you money."

---

# 6. Content Narrative

## Core Narrative
Most technology problems are not technical.
They are failures in communication, alignment, and decision-making.

## Content Pillars
1. **Costly Mistakes**: Highlight common errors that cost money.
2. **Misalignment**: Show the disconnect between business and tech.
3. **Structural Clarity**: Explain how to think better about systems and decisions.
4. **Real Experience**: Share real-world insights and lessons.

---

# 7. Content Principles

- Radical simplicity
- One idea per piece
- Opinion over information
- Focus on impact (money, decisions, risk)
- Create tension
- Repeat strategically
- Speak as an advisor, not an employee

# 8. Visual Identity

## Color System
- Charcoal (#121212) — 70%
- White Smoke (#F5F5F5) — 20%
- Copper (#B7410E) — 8%
- Neon (#D4FF00) — 2%

## Color Usage
- Charcoal: backgrounds
- White: main text
- Copper: strategic emphasis
- Neon: alerts, key insights

## Secondary Colors
- Medium Gray (#2A2A2A)
- Light Gray (#A0A0A0)
- Technical Beige (#D6CFC7)
- Dark Green (#1F3D2B)
- Petroleum Blue (#1B2A34)

## Typography
- Titles: Archivo Black
- Body: Satoshi
- Technical: Fira Code

---

# 9. Visual Style

- Dark backgrounds
- High contrast
- Minimalist layouts
- Structured compositions
- System-like visuals (lines, nodes, connections)

---

# 10. Graphics Guidelines

## Use
- System diagrams
- Flow structures
- Connection lines
- Minimal icons

## Avoid
- Decorative elements without purpose
- Cartoon styles
- Visual clutter

---

# 11. Data Visualization (Charts)

## Rules
- Simple, readable in 3 seconds
- No 3D
- Minimal colors

## Colors
- Background: Charcoal
- Lines: White
- Highlights: Neon
- Secondary: Gray

---

# 12. Photography Style

1. **C-Suite Authority**: Dark background, Rembrandt lighting, serious expression, minimalist clothing.
2. **Strategist in Action**: Screens, data, systems, green/neon accents, analytical posture.
3. **Human Connection**: Warm lighting, subtle smile, approachable but professional.

# 15. Messaging Rules

## Always
- Speak about impact
- Focus on decisions
- Simplify complexity
- Create clarity

## Never
- Over-explain
- Use unnecessary jargon
- Sound generic
- Be purely technical
